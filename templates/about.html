Accident detection

Accident Detection System Our project uses a YOLOv8-based deep learning model to detect and classify road accidents in real-time video streams. The system analyzes live or recorded footage, identifies accident events, and provides instant alerts to enhance road safety and emergency response.

Project Objective

The objective is to develop an AI-powered surveillance system that automatically analyzes Accident footage and detects abnormal behavior patterns to help authorities take timely actions during events or emergencies.

Challenges

Challenges include diverse camera angles, occlusions, low-light conditions, and class imbalance in behavior data. The system must remain robust across multiple real-world scenarios for accurate classification.

Workflow Pipeline

1. Data Collection

Collected accident-related images and videos from Roboflow and other sources, annotated with bounding boxes for accident detection.

2. Preprocessing

Converted videos into frames, resized, and applied augmentation.

3. Model Training

Trained YOLOv8 model in Google Colab with labeled accident data.

4. Training & Tuning

Train using labeled data, fine-tune hyperparameters, and validate using holdout sets.

5. Evaluation

Tested accuracy using mAP, precision, and recall metrics.

6. Deployment

Integrated into a Flask app for real-time accident detection and alerts.
