Car Crash Detection

Our Accident Detection and Reporting System leverages YOLOv8, a state-of-the-art deep learning algorithm, to analyze live or recorded traffic footage. This AI-powered solution automatically detects vehicular crashes, collisions, and accident-prone events in real time. Designed for road safety and emergency response, the system highlights accidents with bounding boxes, assigns confidence scores, and can trigger instant alerts (such as alarms or emails) to notify concerned authorities for quick action.

With the use of real-time video analysis and deep learning, this project contributes to smart transportation systems and enhances road safety by enabling proactive accident detection and emergency response.
